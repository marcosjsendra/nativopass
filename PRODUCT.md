# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NativoPass members in Costa Rica use the mobile experience while deciding where to eat, shop, travel, exercise, relax, or use local services. They need to discover nearby affiliated businesses and understand available benefits quickly. Affiliated businesses are a secondary audience that benefits from exposure and repeat visits.

## Product Purpose

NativoPass is a lifestyle membership ecosystem connecting members with local businesses through discounts, rewards, offers, QR redemption, points, and member-only opportunities. The homepage should make benefits easy to discover and establish the current Figma experience as a stable coded baseline for future design iterations and developer handoff.

## Brand Personality

Vibrant, playful, and local. The product should feel rewarding and energetic while remaining human and grounded in everyday Costa Rican experiences.

## Anti-references

NativoPass must not feel like a generic coupon website, a fintech product, a QR scanner presented as the whole product, or a collection of abstract gift boxes with no human identity. The baseline deliberately preserves the current Figma direction before those issues are addressed in later variants.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the approved baseline before exploring improvements.
- Make the member experience primary while keeping the two-sided ecosystem in view.
- Show the product through recognizable everyday activities and local discovery.
- Keep interactions lightweight, readable, and portable to another frontend stack.
- Preserve every reviewed state so design comparisons remain measurable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text, and reduced-motion support without visually departing from the baseline. Later redesign variants can target broader WCAG 2.2 AA refinements without compromising baseline fidelity.
